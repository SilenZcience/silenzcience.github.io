Build note 001

No backspace. No mercy.

Errors stay on the record exactly where they happened. They are highlighted, counted and unfixable. Accuracy measured this way means something.
My AI told me: "Accuracy with a delete key is just slow perfectionism".

Build note 002

WPM the honest way.

Gross characters divided by five, per elapsed minute is the standard measure since the era of manual typewriters. The timer starts on your first keystroke and stops at the last character.

Build note 001

Four rules, infinite behavior.

Every cell is alive or dead. Conway's B3/S23 rule means that each generation, a dead cell with exactly 3 neighbours is Born, a live cell with 2 or 3 neighbours Survives, everything else dies. From those simple rules you get infinite complexity.

Build note 002

Draw your own experiments.

The grid keeps running while you paint. Drag across cells to seed structure mid-simulation and watch the consequences propagate.

Build note 001

Tea, Rex?

The T-Rex Runner first appeared in September 2014 as an easter egg on Chrome's "No internet" error page: losing your connection sends you back to the prehistoric ages. By 2018 Google reported about 270 million games played per month yet sadly noone has ever finished it. That is because it was tuned so that a flawless endless run would take about 17 million years — precisely how long T. rex walked the Earth.

Build note 002

Gradual difficulty scaling.

The world speed climbs with distance, and once the score clears four hundred, pterodactyls join the game. Gap spacing scales with speed so every obstacle stays theoretically dodgable forever — but once the speed scaling caps out, the gaps keep creeping tighter until their eventual cap is hit.

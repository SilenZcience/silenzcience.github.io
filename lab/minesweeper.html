Build note 001

Fair by construction.

The minefield is placed only after your first reveal, excluding that cell and its neighbourhood. No frustrating loss on the first move.
Empty regions flood-fill iteratively, so large clears stay smooth even on the bigger field. The numbers slowly transition to the signal color the more mines are nearby.

Build note 002

Built to stay sharp at any scale.

The playing field is drawn as vector graphics rather than a raster bitmap, so every cell stays sharp however far you zoom or how dense the grid gets.
Responsive on every level. Leftclick to reveal a position, rightclick to flag it.

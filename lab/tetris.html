Build note 001

7-bag randomizer.

Every piece is drawn from a shuffled seven-piece bag before the bag refills. You will never get the exact same piece more than twice in a row and you will never wait more than 12 turns for any specific piece to return.

Build note 002

7 unique tetrominos.

Tetrominos are geometric pieces made from exactly four squares joined together. The seven distinct shapes are often named by the letters they resemble: I, O, T, J, L, S, and Z. While I, O, and T are axis-symmetric, J and L aswell as S and Z, respectively, are mirror images of each other.

Build note 001

How it works.

The grid is a 6×32 list of on/off switches. A small timing loop runs constantly and, roughly a tenth of a second before each step is due, tells the audio to play whatever cells are switched on. Playing ahead like that keeps the beat steady even if the webpage goes slow.

Build note 002

Voices, hand-built.

Every instrument is synthesized from scratch. The kick is a sine wave that drops pitch fast. The snare layers white noise on top of a triangle tone. The tom is a sine that slides down, lower and slower than the kick. Hats are sharp bursts of filtered noise, and the clap fires three quick noise spikes. Six sounds from one noise buffer, no audio files needed.
